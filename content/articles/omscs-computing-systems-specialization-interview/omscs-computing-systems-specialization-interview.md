## Introducing - [Ryan Denney](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-denney-1418001b9/)

- Software Engineer @ Oracle
- OMSCS specialization in Computing Systems
- Baller 😎

## What were your overall impressions of the OMSCS program?

- Definitely, know what you want to get out of it
- It’s tough! But do not dismay, CoolsterCodes.com is here to lead the way

## What was relevant in OMSCS for work?

- Embedded work
- Operating Systems
- Distributed systems

## What was your least favorite thing about OMSCS?

- Sometimes it was hard to understand what to expect out of a class
- Also sometimes it was tough to juggle work, life and OMSCS at the same time 😅

## What’s your best advice for future students?

- Know your strengths!
- Ease into it

## What was your first class in the program?

- Information security systems

## What was your favorite thing about the OSMCS program?

- Flexibility!!
- This section is great, make sure to watch the video for more details
    - Essentially, it’s not as rigid as some graduate programs which Ryan compares one specifically

## What were your favorite 3 classes at OMSCS?

- Advanced Operating Systems
- [Robotics AI Techniques](/georgia-tech-omscs-ai-for-robotics-review-cs-7638)
- Tied between HCI and Network Science

## Did you like the Computing Specialization?

- Generally yes but they didn’t have many options back in the day
- Now they have HCI and Perception

## Would you choose Computing Systems again if you could go back?

- Maybe, I feel like Machine Learning would have been a more “hot topic” one, but I feel like Computing Systems is always relevant

## Are there any classes you would try to avoid?

- Embedded systems, just because it’s all in Python, and you do only a little work in actual embedded systems
